# 🚚 Supply Chain Performance Analysis

> **Python & Pandas-based supply chain analytics project focused on carrier performance, lead-time variability, route bottlenecks, and fulfillment reliability.**

## 📌 Business Problem

Supply chain delays can increase inventory carrying costs, reduce fulfillment reliability, and negatively affect customer satisfaction.

This project analyzes logistics transaction data to identify operational bottlenecks, evaluate shipping performance, measure lead-time variability, and uncover patterns that can support better logistics and inventory decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze shipping and fulfillment performance
* Identify carrier and route-level bottlenecks
* Measure lead-time variability
* Evaluate shipping delay patterns
* Analyze fulfillment reliability
* Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 🚚 Shipping Mode Bottleneck

Standard shipping accounted for **over 60% of recorded transit delays**, indicating an opportunity to improve fulfillment and staging processes.

### ⏱️ Lead-Time Volatility

High-volume product categories experienced a **22% longer fulfillment window**, highlighting the impact of peak-period warehouse handling.

### 🗺️ Regional Route Inefficiency

Secondary transit lanes connecting Tier-2 distribution hubs showed approximately **1.4× higher delivery-time variability** than primary routes.

### 📦 Delay & Customer Outcomes

Shipments delayed by more than three business days were associated with an **18% increase in order cancellation and return rates**, indicating a relationship between delivery performance and customer outcomes.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Carrier SLA Restructuring

Introduce performance-based carrier agreements with clearly defined service-level thresholds and escalation mechanisms for significant delays.

### 2. Dynamic Safety Stock

Maintain targeted buffer inventory for high-variance SKUs at regional distribution hubs to reduce the impact of unpredictable fulfillment times.

### 3. Automated Staging Alerts

Implement automated monitoring alerts when warehouse staging exceeds predefined thresholds, allowing operations teams to intervene before delays affect final delivery.
